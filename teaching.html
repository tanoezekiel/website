Teaching Philosophy

I believe that effective teaching combines theoretical rigor, intuition, quantitative reasoning, and practical applications. My objective is not only to help students understand formulas and methods, but also to develop their analytical thinking, autonomy, problem-solving abilities, and intellectual confidence.

Because students have different academic backgrounds, I adapt my explanations and teaching pace while maintaining clear learning objectives and high academic standards. I also place considerable importance on regular practice, constructive feedback, and active student participation.

Competitive Examination Preparation

Excellence Prépa

Founder, Coordinator, and Instructor · 2022–2023

Academic initiative helping students from public universities prepare for competitive entrance examinations to ENSEA, INP-HB, and IFORD.

I founded Excellence Prépa, an academic initiative designed to help students from public universities prepare for highly competitive entrance examinations to leading statistical, economic, demographic, and engineering institutions.

I taught mathematics and economics and contributed to the organization of intensive preparation sessions, methodological guidance, exercise correction, mock examinations, and personalized academic support.

The initiative aimed to reduce information and preparation gaps faced by talented students from public universities and to provide them with a structured and demanding learning environment.
