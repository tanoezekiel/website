About Me

I am a PhD student in Economics at the Université de Sherbrooke. My research interests lie at the intersection of development economics, applied econometrics, labor economics, public policy, macroeconomics, and development finance.

Before joining the Université de Sherbrooke, I earned a Master's degree in Public Policy and Development from the Paris School of Economics and an engineering degree in Statistics and Economics from ENSEA Abidjan.

I also serve as a Research Assistant to Dr. Gomez Agou, contributing to research on sovereign debt sustainability, post-HIPC debt dynamics, youth employment, structural transformation, and quantitative methods for development policy.

My broader research agenda seeks to understand how public policy, institutions, human capital, and structural transformation can support inclusive and sustainable development, particularly in Sub-Saharan Africa.
